# Editions and Entitlements

NomoTect defaults to the Apache-licensed community edition. Its essential
capabilities are declared in `config/editions/community.yml` and cannot be
removed by an entitlement provider.

Optional providers may make additional capabilities available. Missing,
unknown or failing providers deny only optional capabilities. They never
disable installation, security, tenancy, design system, grid, domain services,
extensions, upgrades, releases, distribution or operational readiness.

The baseline performs no network calls and requires no license, customer
identifier or commercial token.
