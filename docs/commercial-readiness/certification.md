# Commercial Readiness Certification

The commercial-readiness certification is a fail-closed, local verification of
the community baseline. It proves that every essential community capability is
available when optional providers are missing or raise errors, while unknown
commercial capabilities remain unavailable.

The report also verifies that a fresh installation has no support or customer
relationship and that telemetry is disabled with no selected categories. These
checks keep commercial integrations additive: they may provide optional
capabilities, but they cannot become prerequisites for installation, operation,
security, upgrades, distribution or recovery.

Certification performs no network calls and does not grant an entitlement,
create support consent or enable telemetry. A `passed` report is evidence of
the boundary contract, not permission to publish a stable release.
