Description

This sample demonstrates a fleet-management solution that pairs a Timeline Month Scheduler with a real-time Analytics Chart. The left panel shows driver routes grouped by resource; the right panel delivers analytics for quick insights and decision-making. Key components Timeline Scheduler: Monthly timeline with resource grouping for drivers and routes. Analytics Dashboard: Three switchable views — Trip Count, Truck Capacity, and Longest Trips. Real-time sync: Charts update immediately on event create/edit/delete. Visual consistency: Chart bars inherit scheduler resource colors.
